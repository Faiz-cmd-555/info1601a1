Main Factions

Main faction
Galactic empire
The manifestion of the Emperors power and aurthority over the galaxy. The Empire is a hard hitting yet slow moving faction, their economy and weapons are more powerful than the rest. However their unists can get eassily overwhelmed.
Main faction
Rebel Allaince
The freedom fighters, seperatists, and scoundrels with a sense of honour. The rebels fight to retor the Galactic Republic. The rebels have a weak economy and their units are squishy, but their fighters are the best in the game.
